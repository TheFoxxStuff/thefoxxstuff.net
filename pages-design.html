Story

Started making music in a small bedroom studio in 2019, armed with a cracked DAW and a cheap MIDI keyboard. The first tracks were rough but honest — experiments in layering, texture, and mood.

Over time the setup grew: real synthesizers, acoustic treatment, and an obsession with modular signal processing. The sound became harder to categorize — ambient enough to disappear into, but with enough rhythmic tension to keep you present.

Currently working on the follow-up to Astral Summer, exploring longer-form compositions and live performance setups.
